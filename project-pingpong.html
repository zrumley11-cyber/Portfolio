THE WORK

What I did

A project that let me work across the whole production.

Preproduction

For preproduction, I was responsible for shooting B-Roll, Reserving Equipment, setting up the interview person/date, and getting the talent release form signatures.

Interview day

On interview day, I set up lights and talked to the interviewee.

Postproduction

After the interview, I edited the raw footage of our interviewee into sound bites, cleaned up the audio to improve its quality, found/added the music, and added most of the B-roll to the video.

REFLECTION

What I learned about
telling the story.

When I edited this video, I honestly completely forgot about telling a story. If I could redo the video, I would try to tell a clear story with what the interviewee was saying. Instead, I just found all the good sound bites and threw them together. I would also use different music and get more appealing shots
