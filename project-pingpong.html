BEHIND THE FILM

What I
contributed.

Purpose

I wanted people to see that the org was fun and competitive, and I wanted them to consider joining once they watched.

My role & process

For preproduction, I was responsible for shooting B-Roll, Reserving Equipment, setting up the interview person/date, and getting the talent release form signatures. On interview day, I set up lights and talked to the interviewee. After the interview, I edited the raw footage of our interviewee into sound bites, cleaned up the audio to improve its quality, found/added the music, and added most of the B-roll to the video.

Equipment & editing

For camera equipment, I used a shotgun mic attached to a C-stand to get the audio, I lit the interview using 3-point lighting, and I shot all the footage on a Sony FX-30. I edited this in Adobe Premiere

REFLECTION / 01

What I learned.

When I edited this video, I honestly completely forgot about telling a story. If I could redo the video, I would try to tell a clear story with what the interviewee was saying. Instead, I just found all the good sound bites and threw them together. I would also use different music and get more appealing shots
