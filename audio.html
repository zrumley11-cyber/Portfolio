AUDIO

Audio Story Mix

Project introduction

This is an audio short story, created as a final project for my Audio Production class. The purpose of this project was to tell a story using only audio.

Creative vision

I was trying to tell a humorous story with a good message. I wanted my listeners to hear the message embedded in the story and be encouraged by it.

My role

Most of the sound effects came from my own prerecorded sounds. I used my own voice in this project as well as my friend's. Once I got all the audio, I put it all together and added things such as reverb to the mix that helped to carry the story better.

Challenge

The most challenging part of this edit was when I had to make it sound like I was talking in my head. I did not know how to actually accomplish this task, so I asked my teacher to help me. He showed me what to do, and I was able to have it turn out great!

Equipment and software

I recorded my own sound effects using a Zoom Recorder, and I mixed the whole edit in Pro Tools.

Reflection

One of the most important lessons I took away from this assignment is seeing how valuable audio is to stories.
