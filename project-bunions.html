BEHIND THE FILM

What I
contributed.

About the film

This video was a class project focusing on creating a dialogue-driven short film.

Purpose

The idea behind this video was one hundred percent humor. The goal is to make the viewer laugh the whole time

My role

I personally was the producer of this film: this meant I did all the scheduling of the cast, dates, and talent release forms. I also make sure the equipment was reserved.

Production challenge

One challenging thing was scheduling conflicts: I had lined up some actors who had to change last minute because they could not make the shoot time. I learned that it is hard to schedule people, and early and often communication is key!

Equipment

We used a variety of equipment for this shoot, although I was not behind the camera.

REFLECTION / 01

What I learned.

I am most proud of the way the director and editor were able to make the idea for this comedy come to life.
