Image of God
Documentary

This video explores what it looks like for humans who might look different, have different values, or live in different cultures to all be made in the Image of God. This video was created because we wanted to explore people's different views on the Image of God. We also wanted to encourage people who might doubt their value and worth in the eyes of their Creator.

Project details

Audience experience

I want the viewers to feel warmed and encouraged with a reminder that no matter who they are, they are made in God's image and therefore intrinsically valued.

My role

I was the DP (Director of Photography) for this video. This meant my job was to always man the camera and have the final say in what the shot looked like. I was also responsible for reserving the camera/lighting equipment.

Production challenge

The hardest part of this project was trying to schedule all the interviewees. We originally planned on interviewing 5 different people, but 3 of them fell through. What we ended up doing was cutting the interviews down to 4 people, and even with that, we had to swap out 2 of the people we had originally planned on.

Equipment

For camera equipment, I used a shotgun mic attached to a C-stand to get the audio, I lit the interview using 3-point lighting, and I shot all the footage on a Sony FX-30.

Reflection

The best thing I got out of this project from a production standpoint was more repetition on setting up interviews.
