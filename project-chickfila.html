BEHIND THE FILM

What I
contributed.

Purpose

I wanted the viewers to enjoy watching the video, laugh at the comedy of it, and also buy more Chick-fil-A sauce.

My role & process

I personally did everything in this video: I created a script, got two actors, set up the camera, shot the footage, set up the audio, and edited it all in post. The post-production consisted of working with the audio to get it consistent, layering music in the background, and cutting up the takes to make it flow smoothly. I shot three takes, one with both my actors, and then two other shots over each of their shoulders.

Equipment & editing

I edited with Premiere Pro, used a Sony FX-30 mounted sideways to video vertically, and I used a shotgun mic that was wired into the camera. The mic was attached to a boom pole that was connected to a C-stand. This is the same way I filmed the 3 min promo

REFLECTION / 02

What I learned.

I learned that using a brand logo the whole time would be a good way to use my advertising time to its max. I also learned to take my time when setting up for a shot; I learned to be ok slowing down and making sure the video looks the best it can. I also learned I need to give myself a lot of time to set up before I have my actors come.
